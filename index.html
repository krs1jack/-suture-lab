🏥 Station Overview

🩹 Station 1 — Suturing
  • Simple interrupted suture technique
  • Pre-cut & non-pre-cut wound pads
  • Needle driver, forceps, scissors
  • Suture thread & needles provided
🫀 Station 2 — CPR & Trauma
  • Hands-only CPR: rate, depth, placement
  • Tourniquet application (one-hand method)
  • Gunshot wound packing simulation
  • Splinting (teddy bear leg scenario)
  • Fake blood for realism!

👋 Instructor Introduction — Pick One (~1 Minute)

Start the session with a quick personal intro. Choose whichever prompt feels most natural — or go off-script entirely!

💬
Anything About Yourself Who you are and where you are in your medical journey.
⭐
Best Advice You've Ever Heard Share a piece of advice that has truly stuck with you.
💡
What You Wish You'd Known What do you wish someone had told you before going into medicine?
🎯
Any Quick Insight or Story A moment, lesson, or tip meaningful for students curious about healthcare.
🎤 Keep it to about 1 minute. Authenticity matters more than polish — students connect with the real you.
